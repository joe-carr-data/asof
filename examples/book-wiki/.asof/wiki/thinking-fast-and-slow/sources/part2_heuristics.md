# Part II notes — Heuristics and Biases

## Claims as of source_mtime 2024-04-15

- Three primary System 1 heuristics:
  1. **Anchoring** — arbitrary numbers shown before an estimate bias the estimate.
  2. **Availability** — likelihood judgments default to ease-of-recall, not base rates.
  3. **Representativeness** — probability judged by similarity to stereotypes; produces conjunction-fallacy errors (Linda).
- Heuristics aren't stupid — they work most of the time. The point is knowing when to engage System 2 anyway.
- **Slow down for high-stakes decisions** even when System 1 reports "all fine."
