# Part I notes — Two Systems

## Claims as of source_mtime 2024-03-01

- Kahneman introduces **System 1** (fast, automatic, intuitive) and **System 2** (slow, deliberate, analytical).
- System 1 is always on; System 2 is "lazy" — endorses System 1 outputs unless forced to engage.
- **Cognitive ease**: familiar stimuli → System 1 reports "fine" → System 2 stays disengaged. Branded as WYSIATI ("What You See Is All There Is").
- **Substitution**: System 1 silently swaps a hard question for an easier one.
- Heuristics covered at high level: anchoring, availability, representativeness.
