# System 1

Kahneman's **fast, automatic, intuitive, effortless** mode of thinking. Always on. Produces immediate impressions, gut reactions, and the feeling of "I just know."

## Operates by

- Pattern-matching on familiar stimuli.
- Substitution (silently swapping hard questions for easier ones).
- Heuristics: anchoring, availability, representativeness.

## Failure modes

System 1 doesn't know what it doesn't know. WYSIATI — "What You See Is All There Is" — captures its blind spot: it builds a coherent story from whatever's available and doesn't probe for missing data.
