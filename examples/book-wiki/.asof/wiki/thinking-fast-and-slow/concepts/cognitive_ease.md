# Cognitive ease

The subjective sense that everything is "fine" — produced by [System 1](../entities/system_1.md) when stimuli are familiar, clearly displayed, or repeated.

## Why it matters

Cognitive ease keeps [System 2](../entities/system_2.md) disengaged. The result: System 1's snap judgment gets endorsed without scrutiny.

## Triggers

- Repeated exposure (mere-exposure effect).
- Clear typography / good lighting.
- A primed mood (prior good news → harder to spot the next item's flaws).

## WYSIATI

"What You See Is All There Is" — System 1 builds a coherent story from whatever data is available and doesn't probe for missing data. The combination of cognitive ease + WYSIATI explains why people defend confident judgments based on thin evidence.

## Counter-strategy

Inject friction: change typography, slow the input, or explicitly enumerate what's missing. Any of these can engage System 2.
