# thinking-fast-and-slow — current state

## Snapshot date

2026-05-04 — book club through Part II.

## TL;DR

Kahneman's central frame: two cognitive systems. **System 1** (fast, automatic, intuitive) does most of the work; **System 2** (slow, deliberate, analytical) is "lazy" and endorses System 1 unless forced to engage. Cognitive ease — the felt sense that everything is fine — is what keeps System 2 disengaged. The Part II heuristics (anchoring, availability, representativeness) all exploit cognitive ease.

The book club's recurring takeaway: slow down for high-stakes decisions even when System 1 reports "all fine."
